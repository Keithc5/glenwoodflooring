Specialised & Sports Floor Restoration

We can restore large sports floors, handballs courts, dance floors, gyms or any other type of specialised floor

 

Sports Floors

These floors maybe floors in gyms, handball courts, tennis courts and badminton courts. 

 

As with sports floors they are going to receive considerably more wear and tear than a home floor. For this reason we restore these floors using high performance, long lasting, durable finish. The finish used on sports floors is the Bona Sportive System. Glenwood uses this finish because sports floors require to have a non slip surface. Using the Bona Sportive system you are guaranteed to have a fantastic finish combined with the safety of non slip. 

Other

Floor restoration is also provided for floors such as dance floors, churches, bars and restaurants.

 

We have restored many floors in hotels, some which are dance floors, others which are large wooden floors which greet guests in the lobby. Using our Dust Free system, we restore these floors using odour free varnish. 

 

The dust free system and odour free varnish will ensure that your business will not be affected by any smells or mess, leaving your business with a floor that has been restored to its best.
