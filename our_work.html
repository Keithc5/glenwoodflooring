Our Work

Glenwood with over 20 years experience has restored many floors and has travelled throughout the country.

Some of our work has included many well known establishments.

Glenwood recently restored the main lobby floor in Lough Rynn Castle. This castle floor was over 100 years old when we restored it back to its original beauty.

Some of our other work has involved restoring floors in many hotels bars and restaurants including The Menlo Park, The Merrick hotel in Eyre Square in Galway.

We have restored a large number of sports floors in schools and centres. We have worked recently with the renovation of the Hub centre in Castlerea where we laid a brand new sports floor and also restored a floor in another area of the building.
