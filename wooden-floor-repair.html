Wooden Floor Repair Galway

Glenwood Flooring repairs damaged, scratched and worn wooden floors for homes, businesses and public buildings throughout Galway and across Ireland. Many timber floors can be repaired and restored without the cost and disruption of replacing the full floor.

Fixing Damaged Wooden Floors

Wooden floors can suffer from deep scratches, stains, gaps, loose boards, dull varnish, water marks and heavy wear in busy areas. We assess the condition of the floor, carry out suitable repairs, and prepare the timber for sanding, staining or finishing where needed.

Common Floor Repairs

  • Scratched wooden floor repair
  • Repairing worn or dull timber floors
  • Fixing loose or damaged floorboards
  • Reducing marks, scuffs and stains before sanding
  • Parquet and hardwood floor restoration
  • Preparing repaired floors for varnish, oil or stain

Repair, Sanding and Restoration

A repair is often the first step in restoring an old wooden floor. Once damaged areas are addressed, our dust-free floor sanding process removes old finish, scratches and surface wear. The floor can then be protected with professional floor finishing or changed in colour with wood floor staining.

Wood Floor Repair for Homes and Businesses

We repair and restore timber floors in family homes, hotels, bars, restaurants, churches, gyms, schools and sports facilities. Glenwood Flooring is based in Glenamaddy, County Galway and works with customers throughout Galway and Ireland.

Wooden Floor Repair FAQs

Can scratched wooden floors be repaired?
Yes. Many scratched wooden floors can be repaired by treating damaged areas and sanding the surface before applying a suitable finish.

Is it better to repair or replace a wooden floor?
If the timber is still structurally sound, repair and restoration can often bring the floor back to life at a lower cost than full replacement.

Can you repair old floorboards before sanding?
Yes. Loose, damaged or heavily worn sections can be assessed and repaired before the floor is sanded and refinished.

Do you repair wooden floors in Galway?
Yes. Glenwood Flooring provides wooden floor repair, sanding and restoration services in Galway and throughout Ireland.
