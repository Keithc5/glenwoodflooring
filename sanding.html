Sanding

Glenwood specialises in floor sanding and finishing. We have completed work and restored wooden floors throughout the country. These floors are fully restored to their original beauty. Floor sanding and finishing means you can have a floor that looks like new without the trouble of having to completly replace it. 

 

While sanding floors we use the latest technology. We use dust free sanding machinery that ensures dusting is completed 99% dust free. This will ensure that your floors will be restored in a clean environment leaving your floor restored with no mess. 

 

Our sanding and finishing process is simple and thorough:

  • The floor is sanded back to bare wood
  • Stains, scuffs and scratches are removed
  • The floor is treated and finished to a high standard, leaving it looking as good as new
