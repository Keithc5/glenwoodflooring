Wood Floor Finishing

Glenwood can finish your floor to protect it from every day wear and dirt so that it remains looking like new

 

We finish our floors using only the best finish. This means that your floor will be fully protected from dirt and moisture. The floor finish also gives your floor the final look that keeps it looking sleek and shiny and natural.

Glenwood finish floors using a variety of different products, chosen specifically to suit your floor. These products include Bona, Treatex Hardwax and Sayerlack. These products enhance the look of your floor and ensure that your floors remain easy to clean and stain resistant.

By using only the best finishing products, your floor will remain in excellent condition for many years.
